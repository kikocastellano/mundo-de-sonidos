# Google Play listing — English (en-GB / en-US)

**App name (30):** World of Sounds: Animals
**Short description (80):** Real animal sounds from around the world for kids. No ads, works offline.

**Full description:**
Travel the world and discover what animals sound like! 🦁🐘🐧

🌍 Explore a world map: the farm, the ocean, Africa, America, Asia, Europe, Oceania and the poles.
🔊 152 animals with their real sound, recorded in nature.
🗣️ Hear each name in English and in Spanish — a fun first step into a second language.
📖 Fun facts read out loud: what they eat, where they live, what their babies are called…
👂 "Who's that sound?" and a language game: no lives, no waiting, no frustration.
📒 A sticker album to complete.
🌙 Calm mode: lullaby, rain, waves and more sounds for bedtime.
🧑‍🏫 Teacher mode: slideshows and quizzes for the classroom.

✅ No ads. Ever.
✅ No accounts and no data collection.
✅ Works offline.
✅ For children aged 2 to 8.

Free with dozens of animals. Try everything free for 24 hours. A one-time purchase (no subscription) unlocks every animal, all future ones, calm mode and teacher mode.
